Air Freight

Air Freight

Air Freight represents a significant growth area for OJS-WIN Logistics, and we are constantly enhancing our capabilities. As a member of the prestigious Air Cargo Group (ACG), we benefit from a top-tier international network of air cargo agents around the world. This extensive network allows us to offer air freight services that are both efficient and cost-effective, helping to advance our customers' businesses.

We provide a variety of value-added services, including Ex-Works pickups, door-to-door deliveries, third-country shipments, and road haulage, complementing our core air freight offerings. Additionally, we deliver innovative, bespoke solutions designed to tackle your specific logistics needs.

We are also expanding our Air Import Consolidation services from major international origins to various destinations in India, offering our clients cost-effective and streamlined cargo handling. Furthermore, OJS-WIN Logistics provides multimodal options, such as International Transshipment and Cross-Country solutions, to accommodate a wide range of customer requirements.
