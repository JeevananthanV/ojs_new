Outbound Consolidation (Export Console Segment)

Our Export Consolidation Program is expertly designed to manage LCL shipments of all types from India. We provide LCL services from key ports and ICDs including Chennai, Tuticorin, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Nhavasheva, and Kochi

Our proficient team ensures that LCL cargo is handled with care in our warehouses to prevent damage during offloading and stacking. We meticulously pack and secure each shipment in containers to guarantee safe and reliable transit.

We proudly stand as the leading LCL operator in the country. Our substantial client base and consistent shipment volumes allow us to maintain exceptional schedule accuracy.

Additionally, our specialized Haz Cargo desk streamlines the transport of hazardous materials, and our robust escalation process ensures rapid resolution of any service issues
