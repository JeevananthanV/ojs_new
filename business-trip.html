Gateway to Expand Your Business,

OJS-WIN provides complete China business trip support for businesses looking to explore sourcing opportunities, meet reliable suppliers, and visit major trade fairs such as Canton Fair, Yiwu, Shanghai, and Shenzhen based on specific product categories.

We help clients identify current trending products, connect with dependable suppliers, and enjoy a professional business travel experience that supports confident buying decisions and long-term supplier relationships.

The Canton Fair, started in 1957, is one of China's oldest, largest, and most important international trade fairs.

Organized by the China Foreign Trade Centre and jointly hosted by the Ministry of Commerce of China and the Guangdong Provincial Government, it is held twice every year in April and October. The fair is conducted in 3 phases and covers a wide range of industrial and consumer products. Each year, it attracts thousands of exhibitors and buyers from around the world, making it one of the most influential trade events in China.

Yiwu World International Commodity Fair - Export Goods Exhibition is set to take center stage from October 21 to 24, at Yiwu International Expo Center, Zhejiang, China. Spanning 110,000 square meters with 10,000 international standard booths, this event will gather over 8,900 enterprises, showcasing a diverse range of products. Attendees can explore industries including latest trending products of daily consumer goods, hardware tools, construction hardware, electronics, toys, and mechanical machinery, along with cutting-edge sectors like smart home appliances, 3C digital products, new energy products, and industrial electrical equipment. With 2,00,000+ visiting purchasers expected, it's a prime hub for networking and deals.

Key events add value: the Global New Product Launch Conference reveals trends, the International Competition of Commodity Creative Design highlights creativity, and Trade Negotiations foster partnerships. Don't miss this global trade spectacle.
